2. SEO & Growth Strategy

The "David vs Goliath" Approach

Ranking for "UCAS points calculator" is brutally competitive (dominated by ucas.com and uni websites). Instead of fighting them head-on immediately, we will capture the massive long-tail international market.

  • ✓ Programmatic SEO: Generate hundreds of pages for specific combos (e.g., "UCAS points for A*AB in A-Level").
  • ✓ International Focus: Target keywords like "Convert CAIE A-Levels to US GPA" which are underserved.
  • ✓ Schema Markup: Implement SoftwareApplication and FAQPage schemas so our calculators appear directly in Google search results as rich snippets.

Target Keyword Metrics

A-Level to GPA Converter High Intent / Low KD
UCAS Points Calculator High KD (Dominant)
A-Level requirements for Medicine Medium KD
